Using ChatGPT in Inkwell

Read this notice before connecting. Selecting this provider sends information to OpenAI. Local Ollama generation stays on your machine; API-key providers have their own data handling.

Account and connection information

OpenAI handles sign-in and consent in your system browser. Inkwell processes your verified account identifier, name and email when supplied, connection label, permissions, installation identifier and client registration. It retains these locally so you can select and reconnect accounts. It never requests your ChatGPT password, session cookies or conversation history.

Your story content

When you select a ChatGPT model, requests can contain your instructions, premise, interview and conversation history, outline, selected and surrounding prose, character memory, story bible and running summary, according to the feature and context settings. Requests use your eligible plan allowance or credits. These prompts are sent to OpenAI's public Responses API. Inkwell requests store:false; this is not a promise that OpenAI retains no service data. Review OpenAI's privacy policy and your account controls.

Local storage

Books and settings remain local files. Access, refresh and retained identity tokens are stored in a separate encrypted local credential record using Windows CurrentUser DPAPI. The signed-in Windows user can decrypt it; it is not protection against hostile software running as that user. Tokens are not returned to the browser or kept in browser storage. The browser retains ordinary model preferences and a welcome-message flag. Inkwell adds no analytics for this feature.

Automatic requests require your choice

Automatic ChatGPT summaries are off by default. If you explicitly enable them, leaving a changed chapter may send its text for a running memory summary and use plan allowance. Disable the setting to stop this background activity. Manual generation and manual summaries remain separate actions. Auto-summary settings for other providers are independent.

Disconnecting and limits

Sign out attempts to revoke the renewable session and clears local tokens, retaining the registration and basic account mapping for future sign-in. If remote revocation cannot be confirmed, Inkwell tells you; you can also disconnect the app in ChatGPT settings. Local books are not deleted by sign-out. Model access and usage depend on account eligibility, granted permissions, workspace policy and OpenAI limits; there is no silent switch to API-key billing. Manage usage.

Use this runtime on your own Windows computer and OS account. For redistribution, review the separate SDK license and applicable Sign in with ChatGPT terms. This notice describes application behavior; a distributor must assess its own privacy obligations.
